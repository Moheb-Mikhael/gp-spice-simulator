The Architecture & Development Teams

Arachnode was engineered by an undergraduate team from the Electronics and Electrical Communications Engineering department at Cairo University, developed under the sponsorship and technical mentorship of Si-Vision.

🏆

Founding Engineering Team

The founding team engineered Arachnode from its inception, developing the custom simulation engine, the comprehensive analytics suite, the WebAssembly deployment architecture, and the complete web interface. Every architectural layer was systematically designed, implemented, and rigorously tested by this dedicated group.

Future Development Initiatives

Arachnode is architected for continuous scalability. Future engineering cohorts will expand the simulation engine with advanced device models, specialized analysis methodologies, enhanced visualization tools, and extended evaluation capabilities—building upon the robust foundation established by the founding team.
