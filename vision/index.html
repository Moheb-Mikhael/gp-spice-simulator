Our Vision

Why we built this, and where we’re going.

“Fortunately, science, like that nature to which it belongs, is neither limited by time nor by space. It belongs to the world, and is of no country and of no age.” — Ahmed H. Zewail, quoting Sir Humphrey Davy, Nobel Banquet 1999

We want every Egyptian engineering student to see that EDA is not a black box. You can understand it. You can improve it. You can build the next generation of tools that the world’s engineers will rely on.

Built by students, for students. Share it with your classmates, extend it, and make it yours.
This is just the beginning.
