About the Tool

A complete SPICE post-processing suite for Web, Windows, and Linux.

📊

Waveform Database

Load, inspect, and manage simulation data with a built-in WaveformDB. List signals, view metadata, and switch between multiple datasets seamlessly.

⚙

Measurement Engine

Measure rise time, fall time, period, frequency, delay, peak-to-peak, RMS, average, and more. Window your analysis with from … to.

⭐

Built-In Calculator

Evaluate arithmetic expressions on waveforms. Supports +, -, *, /, trigonometric functions, logarithms, and reduction functions.

📝

Export & Plot

Export data to CSV, generate publication-ready plots with custom titles, and save as PNG. All computation runs locally via WebAssembly.

🖥

Cross-Platform

Use the desktop app on Windows or Linux for native performance, or access the full post-processing suite directly in your browser via WebAssembly. Same engine, every platform.

🏫

Open Source

Study every layer — from the netlist parser to the plotting engine. Contribute, modify, and build your own EDA tools on top of it.

Our Vision

Why we built this, and where we’re going.

“Electronic Design Automation is the backbone of modern technology — yet the tools that shape it are rarely built by the engineers who will use them tomorrow. We believe Egyptian students can and should be part of that conversation.”

This platform is our first step. A fully open-source SPICE simulation and post-processing environment that runs on Web, Windows, and Linux, requires nothing to install, and invites you to look under the hood.

We want every Egyptian engineering student to see that EDA is not a black box. You can understand it. You can improve it. You can build the next generation of tools that the world’s engineers will rely on.

Contribute on GitHub. Share it with your classmates. Fork it, break it, fix it, make it yours.
This is just the beginning.
